# Community standards

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique code and claims, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and publication of private information are not acceptable in project discussions or contributions.

Use minimal, permissioned examples. Do not pressure others to disclose private source, credentials, personal information, or customer data to reproduce a problem.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to **info@epistemic.ai**, using the subject **Diffstory community**. Reports will be handled with attention to privacy.
